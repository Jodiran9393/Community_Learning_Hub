Large Language Models

Redefining intelligence and human-computer interaction

What are LLMs?

Large Language Models are AI systems trained on vast amounts of text data. They can understand, generate, and reason with human language, powering applications like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

Key Concepts

  • Transformers: The architecture behind modern LLMs
  • Attention Mechanism: How models focus on relevant information
  • Tokens: The building blocks of text for LLMs
  • Fine-tuning: Customizing models for specific tasks
  • Embeddings: Numerical representations of text
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Combining LLMs with external knowledge
